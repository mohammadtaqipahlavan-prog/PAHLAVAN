Discover Iranian Dates

Different varieties offer different textures, sweetness, appearance and storage characteristics. Tell us what your market needs, and we can discuss the relevant product details.

01
Bam · Iran

Mazafati Dates

Dark, soft and moist dates with a rich, sweet taste. Mazafati is one of Iran's best-known date varieties and is closely associated with the Bam region.

Soft · Moist · Rich Ask about this ↗
02
Southern Iran

Piarom Dates

Elongated, dark dates with a relatively thin skin and semi-dry texture. Often considered for premium presentation and selected date assortments.

Semi-Dry · Distinctive Ask about this ↗
03
Iran

Zahedi Dates

Golden to light-brown dates with a firmer texture and a comparatively mild sweetness. A versatile option for different food applications and markets.

Firm · Mildly Sweet Ask about this ↗
04
Southern Iran

Kabkab Dates

A soft, sweet variety associated with southern Iran. Kabkab dates are known for their fleshy texture and are used in a range of traditional food settings.

Soft · Sweet Ask about this ↗
05
Southeastern Iran

Rabbi Dates

An elongated, dark, semi-dry date associated with southeastern Iran. Its appearance and texture make it a variety worth considering for different market needs.

Semi-Dry · Elongated Ask about this ↗
06
Khuzestan · Iran

Sayer Dates

Also known as Estamaran, this commercially important Iranian variety has a relatively firm texture and is used in wholesale and food-processing markets.
